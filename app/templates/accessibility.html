Accessibility statement

This application is designed to conform to Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (36 CFR 1194), which incorporates the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA, and targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page has a unique title, a "Skip to main content" link, and header, navigation, main and footer landmarks.
  • All features work with a keyboard alone. Focus is always visible and follows a logical order.
  • All form fields have visible labels. Hints and error messages are announced by screen readers.
  • When a form has errors, a summary appears at the top with links to each field, and receives focus.
  • Status is never shown by color alone: every status tag and alert includes text.
  • Text and interface components meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for components.
  • Data tables have captions and header cells with scope.
  • Pages reflow to a 320-pixel width and support 200% text zoom and custom text spacing without loss of content.
  • Before your session times out, you are warned and can extend it (WCAG 2.2.1).
  • Animations are minimal and respect your "reduce motion" setting.
  • The core application is rendered on the server and works without JavaScript.

Known limitations

  • Receipt uploads are not yet available; a checkbox records whether a receipt is attached.
  • The permission matrix is a large grid. A role-by-role editor is provided as an alternative.

Feedback

If you have difficulty using any part of this site, contact your Defense Travel Administrator and describe the page, the task, and the assistive technology you use.
